# Security

Do not report a security problem in a public issue. Anyone can read those,
including people who would use the problem against other customers.

Report it privately in one of these ways:

- On this repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**.
  Only the MCServerHost team can read the report.
- Email [support@mcserverhost.com](mailto:support@mcserverhost.com) with
  "Security" in the subject line.

Tell us what you found, the steps to reproduce it and what someone could do
with it. We reply to every report and tell you when the problem is fixed.

Test only against your own account and your own servers. Do not access other
customers' data, and do not run tests that slow down or stop the service for
anyone else.
